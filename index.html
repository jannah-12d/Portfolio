Who am I?

I’m a Product Designer 2 at noon, where I lead design for my squad’s new seller app, bringing multiple seller tools into one experience. I built its design system and the custom Claude design skills that help us scale it. Before that, I was the main designer for the DirectShip app and the DirectShip experience in Seller Lab. My roots are in graphic design: I studied at the German University in Cairo and spent four years shaping a jewelry brand’s identity, packaging and campaigns. It’s why I care as much about type, color and detail as I do about flows.
