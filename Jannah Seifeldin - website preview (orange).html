Mobile app · Design system

Role
Product Designer
Platform
Mobile
Year
2026

Design system and custom Claude design skills

The noon Partners app is noon’s first all-in-one mobile app for sellers, bringing the Seller Lab experience and everything sellers need into one place. It saves sellers time, simplifies their day-to-day processes, and lets them run their business without being tied to a desk, adding real value for both sellers and noon. My team took on the move from web to app, and I designed it from scratch through to launch. I drafted the full app in Figma as wireframes and built a new design system. I then created a custom Claude skill and used it to turn those drafts into the complete UI, which sped up both my design work and the front-end team’s build.
